# AgentScale — Marketing Plan

## Positioning

**Category:** AI Workforce Management  
**Tagline:** The System of Record for AI Workers  
**Tone:** Corporate, minimal, trustworthy — Workday × Linear × Vanta

## ICP (ideal customer profile)

- **Company size:** 500–10,000 employees
- **Signals:** 10+ production or pilot agents; regulated industry or high customer-facing AI
- **Buyer:** Head of AI / CTO + CISO or Compliance sponsor
- **Champion:** Platform engineering or AI ops lead

## Messaging pillars

1. **Inventory** — Know every agent, owner, and risk level
2. **Readiness** — Onboard and train before production
3. **Proof** — Scenario testing + certification gates
4. **Control** — Audit trail for every prompt and model change
5. **Leadership** — Executive dashboard for compliance posture

## Channels (MVP phase)

| Channel | Tactic |
|---------|--------|
| **Website** | Landing at agentscale.vercel.app; demo CTA |
| **Outbound** | Design partner programme (3–5 logos) |
| **Content** | "AI worker lifecycle" thought leadership |
| **Product** | Demo data mode for instant product tour |
| **Email** | Resend for contact + onboarding sequences |

## Launch phases

### Phase 1 — Stealth / design partners
- Private demos; no broad paid ads
- Case study template: before/after agent governance

### Phase 2 — Public beta
- Self-serve signup + Starter plan
- Webinar: "Certifying agents for production"

### Phase 3 — Enterprise
- SSO, audit export, compliance packs
- Partner integrations (Langfuse, ITSM)
