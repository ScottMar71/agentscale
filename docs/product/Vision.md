# AgentScale — Vision

> **The System of Record for AI Workers.**

## North star

Enterprises are deploying AI agents faster than they can **onboard, train, certify, govern, and optimise** them. AgentScale is the platform that makes AI workers as manageable as human employees — with registry, lifecycle, compliance, and executive visibility in one place.

## Problem

- Agents sprawl across teams with no single inventory
- Prompt and model changes lack audit trails
- No standard path from pilot → production certification
- Leadership cannot see risk, coverage, or ROI

## Solution

A multi-tenant SaaS platform that provides:

1. **Agent Registry** — canonical record of every AI worker
2. **Onboarding & Academy** — structured readiness and training
3. **Scenario Testing** — AI-assisted evaluation against rubrics
4. **Certifications** — policy-gated approval to deploy
5. **Governance** — audit logs, versioning, incidents
6. **Executive Dashboard** — org-wide health and compliance posture

## Who we serve

| Segment | Need |
|---------|------|
| **AI / Platform teams** | Registry, versioning, evaluation |
| **Risk & Compliance** | Audit trail, certification gates |
| **Business owners** | Agent ownership, department roll-up |
| **Executives** | Dashboard, limits, plan enforcement |

## Principles

- **Trustworthy** — corporate tone (Workday / Linear / Vanta)
- **Multi-tenant by default** — org isolation via Supabase RLS
- **Demo-ready, pilot-capable** — ship MVP in 8–12 weeks
